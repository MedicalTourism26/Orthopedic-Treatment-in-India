**Understanding Orthopedic Treatment**

Orthopedic care covers a wide range of conditions involving the musculoskeletal system. Depending on the patient's problem, the specialist may evaluate X-rays, MRI or CT scans, blood investigations, physical findings, and previous treatment records.

Treatment is not the same for every patient. Depending on the diagnosis, care may include medication, physiotherapy, rehabilitation, injections, minimally invasive procedures, or surgery such as joint replacement or fracture-related procedures when clinically required.
